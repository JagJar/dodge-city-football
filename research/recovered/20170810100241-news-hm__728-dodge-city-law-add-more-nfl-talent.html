Dodge City Law Add More NFL Talent

Category: News Written by Joi Ponder | Dodge City Law
fShare
Save

With the signing of Brandon Venson, Clayton Callicutt, Travis Taylor and Ron Fields the Dodge City Law now boast a roster of 6 players that have been on NFL rosters along with previously signed Tank Tyler and Donnell Alexander of the Kansas City Chiefs. Head Coach Sean Ponder says, “We are putting together a very talented roster with players that are trying to move up and hungry to prove it, obviously, there will be a lot of eyes on us from NFL teams with these players and that’s a great thing for them as well as the other players on our team trying to move up.”

Brandon Venson 6’4” 230 WR/ NFL Chicago Bears
Returning to the LAW this season with 96 Rec for 1163 Yards and 19 TD’s in his career with the Law.

Ron Fields 6’3” 190 DB Texas A&M Commerce/ NFL Dallas Cowboys. Was in camp with the Cowboys after having 98 tackles 5Int and 22 PBU at Texas A&M Commerce.

Clayton Callicutt 6’4” 285 Angelo State/ NFL Chicago Bears
The Division 2 All American at Angelo State had 20 sacks in his career there and was signed by the
Chicago Bears.

Travis Taylor 6’2” 230 LB Murray State/ NFL Tampa Bay Bucs
In 2 years at Murray State Taylor had 165 tackles 23.5 tackles for a loss and 7.5 sacks.
