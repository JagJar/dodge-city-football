2017 season at a glance

The Law opened 2017 with five straight wins — including a 59–52 home win over the Duke City Gladiators on March 4 and road wins at Wichita and Salina — and sat 6–1 in late April when the Kiowa County Signal profiled a possible record season under head coach Sean Ponder. Rookie quarterback Rudy Johnson (Winston-Salem State) led an offense averaging more than 54 points per game at that stage.

The archived schedule records home wins over Salina (87–42), Dallas (56–19), Wichita (62–39), Duke City (65–62) and Texas (49–46), road wins at Wichita, Salina and Kansas City, and a first-round playoff exit at Texas. See every date, venue and score in the full schedule.
