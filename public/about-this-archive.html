DODGE CITY LAW · HISTORICAL ARCHIVE

About This Archive

This is an unofficial historical restoration of the Dodge City Law’s website. The team played from 2014 through 2017. This archive is not affiliated with the former team, ownership, league, or sponsors.

Restoration reference

The desktop layout follows the June 2, 2017 homepage: a 1170-pixel layout, Titillium Web typography, overlapping team logo, yellow navigation, and photographic panels. Nearby 2017 captures supply missing assets and article text. Mobile layouts have been adapted for readability and keyboard access.

What the dates mean

The homepage standings are the June 2 snapshot. The 24-player roster is preserved in the July 12 capture. News dates are publication dates taken from the original headline list; capture dates can be later. The schedule contains later June results and is labeled separately. Where the earlier rebuild did not preserve an exact response timestamp, we say so.

Recovered and incomplete material

Four team announcements are restored from complete captures; the former-Chiefs announcement has a surviving excerpt. The Kiowa County Signal article is presented as an attributed summary. Player detail pages reconstruct the captured roster fields, not unverified biographies. Pages with missing text are marked partial.

Historical services

Ticket offers, telephone numbers, registrations, and requests belong to the historical record. This site accepts no purchases or submissions. Old social widgets and score services have been replaced with local content or labeled archive links.

Source inventory

Only pre-2018 captures are included. Known later parked-domain material is excluded. Images and fonts are served locally; visiting an explicit source link opens the Internet Archive.
