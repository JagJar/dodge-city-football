  • Law set for big season — April 24, 2017 (via Kiowa County Signal, by Timothy Everson).

    The Law started out with their best record in years and hoped to win a championship in 2017. At 6–1, no Law team had ever won more than eight games in a season; head coach Sean Ponder targeted a one or two seed and a home playoff game. The offense ranked fifth in the CIF in points per game (about 54) and third in yards per game (287.1). Rookie quarterback Rudy Johnson ranked top-five in passing yards per game (181.4) with 25 passing touchdowns against 6 interceptions, plus a team-leading 232 rushing yards. Ponder credited the offensive line for the protection that made the offense go. (Summary of the archived article body; byline and outlet preserved.)
