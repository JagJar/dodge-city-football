About This Archive

This is an unofficial historical tribute to the Dodge City Law (Dodge City, Kansas; inaugural season 2014; Champions Professional Indoor Football League, then Champions Indoor Football; last season 2017). It is a total rebuild from Internet Archive Wayback Machine captures of dodgecityfootball.com — not a copy of the original server, and not affiliated with any former owner, league, or sponsor.

Baseline & cutoff

  • Baseline: homepage capture 20170602095555 of http://dodgecityfootball.com/ (Joomla! site, “shaper_sportson” template, Titillium Web type, yellow header).
  • Cross-checks: schedule.html, team/roster.html, and news-hm/731-law-set-for-big-season.html from the same June 2017 capture window; homepage captures run continuously through 20170919144156, then gap to 20181228124524.
  • Cutoff: everything dated 2018-01-01 or later excluded — post-2017 captures under this domain are parked-domain spam (repair-manual PDFs), verified in the CDX listing.

What was vendored vs. rewritten

  • Vendored: assets/img/dodge-city-law-logo.png (from /images/dodge-city-law-logo.png, June 2017 capture). All other imagery was reference-only.
  • Rewritten: all pages, stylesheets and tables are new static source following the archive's information architecture. No Joomla code, trackers, ad scripts, social widgets, or video embeds were carried over. No page hotlinks to web.archive.org.
  • Research copies kept alongside the build: research/baseline-20170602.html, research/schedule-2017.html, research/news-731.html, research/roster.html.
